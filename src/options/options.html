screink options

Changes are saved as you make them.

UI language

Reading URLs written as text

A URL written as text is not always read correctly: how large it is on screen and what it says both matter. What comes back can be a URL that does not exist, or one that leads somewhere else entirely.

See what was read

With this ON, what the “See what was read” button shows — the button on the screink panel that appears whenever a tab is not opened directly — comes up every time. Keep it OFF if you would rather not have tabs switch during a meeting.

Keyboard shortcut

You can assign a keyboard shortcut of your choosing for entering aim mode. None is assigned at the moment.

Privacy

  • Everything happens inside your browser. No image and no URL is ever sent anywhere.
  • Captured images, what was read, and where you pointed are all discarded. The only thing saved is the settings on this page.
  • A single frame is captured at the moment you confirm a position — never continuously.
