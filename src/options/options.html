screink options

Changes are saved as you make them.

When something will not read

You can open it any time from the panel that appears after reading, so leaving this off loses nothing. Keep it off if you would rather not have tabs switch during a meeting.

Keyboard shortcut

You can assign a keyboard shortcut of your choosing for entering and leaving aim mode. None is assigned at the moment.

Privacy

  • Everything happens inside your browser. No image and no URL is ever sent anywhere.
  • Captured images, what was read, and where you pointed are all discarded. The only thing saved is the settings on this page.
  • A single frame is captured at the moment you confirm a position — never continuously.
